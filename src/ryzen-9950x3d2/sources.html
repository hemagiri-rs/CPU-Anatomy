Sources and caveats

A teaching figure, not a die photo. Chip specs come from launch reviews. The core's structures come from AMD's own Hot Chips 2024 presentation. Die and package layouts are simplified. The working-set explorer is our own model: one core reading uniformly at random, with each cache holding its share of the data. That's the worst case for a cache, so real programs do better. Latencies are rounded published measurements: L3 at 46 cycles comes from a mobile Zen 5 part, plus the 4 cycles that V-Cache adds, and DRAM at 70 ns comes from a 9950X with DDR5-6000.

  • AMD · Next Generation “Zen 5” Core, Hot Chips 2024 (PDF): two 4-wide decode pipes, 6K op cache, 2 taken predictions per cycle, 16K L1 BTB, 52-entry return stack, 8-wide dispatch, 448-entry ROB, 6 ALUs, 4 AGUs, 4 FP pipes with a 512-bit datapath, 240 integer and 384 vector registers, 96-entry FP queue, 48 KB L1D at 4 cycles, 1 MB L2
  • Chips and Cheese · AMD's 9800X3D: 2nd Generation V-Cache: the 64 MB SRAM die moved beneath the CCD, the fabric link now runs down through it, and V-Cache adds 4 cycles to an L3 hit
  • Chips and Cheese · Zen 5's op cache and clustered decoder: one decode pipe per thread, so 4 a cycle for one thread and 8 for two from the decoders alone
  • Chips and Cheese · Ryzen 9950X: Zen 5 on desktop: two CCDs and one I/O die, Infinity Fabric per CCD, about 70 ns DRAM latency with DDR5-6000
  • StorageReview · Ryzen 9 9950X3D2 Dual Edition review: 16 cores and 32 threads, 4.3 to 5.6 GHz, 16 MB L2 + 192 MB L3 = 208 MB, 200 W TDP and 270 W PPT, 360 mm cooler recommended, V-Cache on both CCDs
  • Wikipedia · Ryzen 9 9950X3D: release on April 22, 2026
  • Wikipedia · Zen 5: CCD of 70.6 mm², 32 MB L3 per CCD and 96 MB with V-Cache, I/O die on TSMC N6
  • HWCooling · Zen 5 tested: L2 at 14 cycles, L3 at 46 cycles on a mobile part
  • Wikipedia · Socket AM5: LGA 1718
  • Tom's Hardware · Ryzen 7000 platform and ServeTheHome · Ryzen 7000 platform overview: the 6 nm, 122 mm² I/O die the 9000 series reuses, 28 PCIe 5.0 lanes, DDR5 and the 2-CU RDNA 2 graphics
