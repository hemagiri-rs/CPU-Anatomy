Sources and caveats

A teaching figure, not a die photo. The tile list, core counts, caches, NPU and process nodes come from Intel's disclosures as reported below. Block positions on the compute tile are simplified from published annotated die shots. Structure sizes inside the P-core are Lion Cove's published figures, which Intel says Cougar Cove extends. The cycle model is our own small simulator: the widths, port counts and cache latencies follow those figures, and everything else is simplified.
